## High-priority always

Any medication is high-priority by default. The cost of missing a dose ranges from minor (joint supplement) to severe (insulin, anticonvulsants, heart medications). When in doubt, schedule medications as priority=high.

## Daily medications — same time each day

Daily medications generally need to be given at the same time(s) each day. Pair them with an existing daily anchor (morning feed, evening feed) to make the routine stick. Duration is typically 1-3 minutes per dose.

## Monthly preventives

Heartworm, flea, tick, and combination preventives are almost always given monthly. Pick a memorable day (1st of the month, payday) and stick with it. Duration is 1-2 minutes.

## Quarterly and longer-interval treatments

Some newer preventives (Bravecto, ProHeart) last 3 months or longer. Frequency=monthly is wrong for these — they should be scheduled at their actual interval, not the default.
