## Hot weather

Pavement temperatures can reach 120-140F when air temperature is 85F. Walking on hot pavement burns paw pads. In hot weather, schedule walks before 9am or after 7pm and shorten duration by 30-50%. Brachycephalic breeds (Bulldog, Pug) should not be walked above 75F.

## Cold weather

Most dogs tolerate cold above freezing fine, but small dogs, short-coated dogs, and seniors benefit from coats below 45F. Salt and ice melt are toxic if licked from paws — wipe paws after winter walks (adds 2-3 min to the task).

## Post-meal exercise (bloat risk)

Deep-chested breeds (Great Dane, Standard Poodle, Weimaraner, German Shepherd, Doberman) should not exercise vigorously within 1 hour before or after meals. This is a top cause of gastric dilatation-volvulus (GDV / bloat), which is fatal without emergency surgery. When scheduling: separate "Feeding" and "Walk" tasks for these breeds by at least 60 minutes.

## Conflict-aware scheduling

When generating a daily plan, avoid placing high-intensity exercise immediately adjacent to feeding for any large breed. A walk that ends 5 minutes before feeding is functionally a conflict even if PawPal's overlap detector says it isn't.
