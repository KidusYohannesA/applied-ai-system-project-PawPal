## Annual wellness exam

Healthy adult dogs and cats need an annual wellness exam — physical, weight check, dental check, and core vaccine boosters as scheduled. The visit itself is typically 30-60 minutes, scheduled as a one-time task per year. Recommended frequency=once (per year), priority=high, duration=60 min.

## Semi-annual exams for seniors

Senior dogs (7+ for large breeds, 10+ for small) and senior cats (10+) benefit from semi-annual exams. Subtle changes in weight, kidney values, or thyroid levels are easier to catch every 6 months than every 12. Recommended frequency=once (per 6 months), priority=high, duration=60 min.

## Dental cleanings

Most dogs and cats need a professional dental cleaning under anesthesia every 1-3 years depending on breed (small breeds and brachycephalics typically need them more often). This is a one-time scheduled appointment. Recommended frequency=once (per year or two), priority=high.

## Vaccinations

Core vaccines are typically given on schedules of 1 year or 3 years depending on the vaccine. Track the actual due date for each vaccine rather than scheduling generic "vaccination" tasks. Recommended frequency=once (per due date), priority=high.
