## Urgent signs that override the schedule

Any of the following warrants skipping scheduled tasks and calling a vet: difficulty breathing, repeated vomiting, bloated/distended abdomen, collapse, suspected toxin ingestion, eye injury, inability to urinate (especially in male cats — can be fatal within 24-48 hours), seizures, or severe lethargy. The schedule should not push a pet through these.

## Subtle changes worth a check-in task

Any of the following are reasons to schedule an "observe / check in with vet" task within the next few days: appetite change lasting more than 2 days, water intake change, weight change, limping, scratching more than usual, behavior changes (hiding, irritability), bad breath, or coat changes. These are typically priority=medium tasks of 5-15 minutes.
