## Brushing — short-coated breeds

Short-coated breeds (Beagle, Boxer, Boston Terrier, Pug, Dalmatian) need brushing about once a week with a bristle brush or rubber mitt. Each session takes 10-15 minutes. Recommended frequency=weekly, priority=medium, duration=15 min.

## Brushing — double-coated breeds

Double-coated breeds (Husky, German Shepherd, Golden Retriever, Akita, Shiba Inu, Australian Shepherd) need brushing 2-3 times per week with an undercoat rake, escalating to daily during seasonal "blowing coat" periods (spring and fall). Each session takes 20-30 minutes. Recommended frequency=weekly (or more), priority=medium, duration=20-30 min.

## Brushing — long-coated breeds

Long-coated breeds (Maltese, Yorkshire Terrier, Shih Tzu, Lhasa Apso, Afghan Hound) need daily brushing to prevent painful matting, especially behind the ears, under the legs, and around the collar line. Each session is 10-20 minutes. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=medium, duration=15 min.

## Bathing

Most dogs need a bath every 4-8 weeks. Bathing more frequently than every 2 weeks strips skin oils and can cause irritation. A bath including drying takes 30-45 minutes for medium dogs. Recommended frequency=monthly, priority=low-medium, duration=30-45 min.

## Nail trimming

Nail trimming is needed every 3-4 weeks for most dogs. Overgrown nails change gait and can cause joint pain. Each trim takes about 10-15 minutes. Recommended frequency=monthly, priority=medium, duration=10-15 min.

## Teeth brushing

Daily teeth brushing is the gold standard; 2-3 times per week is the practical minimum to slow dental disease. Each brushing takes 3-5 minutes. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=medium, duration=5 min.

## Ear cleaning

Floppy-eared breeds (Cocker Spaniel, Basset Hound, Beagle, Labrador Retriever) need ear checks weekly and gentle cleaning every 2-4 weeks. Erect-eared breeds need only periodic checks. Recommended frequency=weekly check / monthly cleaning, priority=medium, duration=5-10 min.
