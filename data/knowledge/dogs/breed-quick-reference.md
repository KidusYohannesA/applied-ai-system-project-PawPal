## Shiba Inu

Medium-energy spitz-type breed. Adult exercise: 45-60 min/day, ideally split. Double-coated, blows coat heavily twice a year — brushing 2-3x/week normally, daily during shed seasons. Independent and food-motivated; benefits from daily 5-10 min training sessions. Typical lifespan 12-15 years.

## Labrador Retriever

High-energy retriever. Adult exercise: 60-90 min/day with retrieving or swimming if possible. Prone to obesity — strict portion control on twice-daily feeding. Short coat, weekly brushing. Strong food motivation makes daily training easy. Typical lifespan 10-12 years.

## Golden Retriever

High-energy retriever. Adult exercise: 60-90 min/day. Long double coat — brushing 3-4x/week, daily during shed seasons. Floppy ears need weekly checks for infection. Highly social, suffers from being left alone for long periods. Typical lifespan 10-12 years.

## German Shepherd

High-energy working breed. Adult exercise: 60-120 min/day plus daily mental work (training or scent games). Double coat, brushing 2-3x/week. Deep-chested — separate feeding and exercise by at least an hour to reduce bloat risk. Typical lifespan 9-13 years.

## Border Collie

Very high-energy herding breed. Adult exercise: 90-120+ min/day, must include problem-solving. Will create work for itself (often destructive) without daily mental enrichment. Medium coat, brushing 2x/week. Typical lifespan 12-15 years.

## French Bulldog

Low-energy brachycephalic. Adult exercise: two 15-20 min walks/day, never above 75F outdoors. Short coat, weekly brushing, but skin folds need wiping every 2-3 days. Typical lifespan 10-12 years.

## Chihuahua

Low-energy toy breed. Adult exercise: 20-30 min/day across two short walks. Sensitive to cold (coat below 45F). Tendency toward dental disease — daily teeth brushing strongly recommended. Typical lifespan 14-17 years.

## Beagle

Medium-energy hound. Adult exercise: 45-60 min/day, scent walks especially valuable. Short coat, weekly brushing. Floppy ears need weekly checks. Strong food drive, prone to obesity. Typical lifespan 12-15 years.

## Poodle (Standard)

Medium-high energy. Adult exercise: 60+ min/day. Curly coat needs daily brushing and professional grooming every 6-8 weeks. Deep-chested — separate meals and exercise. Typical lifespan 12-15 years.

## Yorkshire Terrier

Medium-energy toy breed. Adult exercise: 30 min/day across two walks. Long single coat needs daily brushing if kept long, or trimmed short for easier care. Prone to dental issues. Typical lifespan 13-16 years.

## Husky

Very high-energy working breed. Adult exercise: 90-120+ min/day, must include vigorous activity. Heavy double coat, brushing 2-3x/week, daily during shed seasons. Heat-intolerant. Typical lifespan 12-15 years.

## Pug

Low-energy brachycephalic toy. Adult exercise: two 15 min walks/day, avoid heat. Heavy shedder — weekly brushing. Skin folds need cleaning every 2-3 days. Typical lifespan 12-15 years.
