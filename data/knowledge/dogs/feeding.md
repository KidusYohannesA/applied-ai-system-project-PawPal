## Adult dog meal frequency

Most adult dogs (1-7 years) do best on two meals per day — typically 12 hours apart. A single daily meal increases the risk of bloat in deep-chested breeds (Great Dane, Standard Poodle, Weimaraner). Each feeding takes about 10-15 minutes including prep and supervision. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=high, duration=10-15 min per meal.

## Puppy meal frequency

Puppies under 6 months need 3-4 meals per day; puppies 6-12 months need 3 meals; after 12 months most can transition to 2 meals. Skipping meals in young puppies risks hypoglycemia, especially in toy breeds. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=high.

## Senior dog feeding

Senior dogs (7+ years for large breeds, 10+ for small breeds) typically still benefit from twice-daily feeding, but portion sizes often need to drop 20-30% to match reduced activity. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=high, duration=10-15 min.

## Fresh water

Fresh water should be available at all times and the bowl rinsed and refilled at least once daily. This is a quick task, around 2-3 minutes. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=high, duration=2-5 min.
