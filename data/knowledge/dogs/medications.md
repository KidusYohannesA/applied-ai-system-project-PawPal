## Heartworm prevention

Heartworm prevention is given on the same day every month, year-round in most US regions. Missing a dose by more than ~2 weeks can break protection. Administration takes 1-2 minutes. Recommended frequency=monthly, priority=high, duration=2 min.

## Flea and tick prevention

Topical and oral flea/tick preventives are typically given monthly; some newer chewables last 3 months. Year-round dosing is recommended even in cold climates because indoor heating sustains flea populations. Recommended frequency=monthly, priority=high, duration=2 min.

## Daily prescription medications

Daily prescription medications (thyroid, seizure, heart, allergy) usually need to be given at the same time(s) each day to keep blood levels stable. Even a few hours of drift can affect efficacy for narrow-window drugs like phenobarbital. Each administration is 1-3 minutes. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=high, duration=2-3 min.

## Joint supplements

Glucosamine, omega-3, and similar joint supplements are typically given daily with food. Effects build over 4-6 weeks of consistent use; sporadic dosing provides little benefit. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=medium, duration=1-2 min.
