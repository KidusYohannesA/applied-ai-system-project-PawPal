## Daily training sessions

Short daily training sessions (5-10 minutes) are far more effective than one long weekly session. Dogs learn best in brief, focused bursts. Working and herding breeds especially benefit from daily mental work to avoid behavioral problems. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=medium, duration=10 min.

## Puzzle feeders and food enrichment

Puzzle feeders, lick mats, and snuffle mats turn a meal into 15-30 minutes of mental stimulation. Recommended for any dog showing boredom signs. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=medium, duration=20 min.

## Sniff walks (decompression walks)

Letting a dog choose the route and sniff freely for 30-45 minutes provides as much mental tiredness as a much longer power walk. Especially valuable for high-energy breeds and reactive dogs. Recommended frequency=weekly+, priority=medium, duration=30-45 min.

## Socialization for puppies

Puppies in the 3-16 week socialization window need positive exposure to new people, sounds, surfaces, and (vaccinated) dogs. Missing this window correlates with adult fear and reactivity. Recommended frequency=daily during this window, priority=high, duration=15-20 min.
