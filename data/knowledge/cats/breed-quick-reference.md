## Domestic Shorthair

Variable energy. 2-3 play sessions/day of 10-15 min. Short coat, weekly brushing. Standard nail trims monthly. Most adaptable to busy households. Typical lifespan 13-17 years.

## Maine Coon

Medium-energy large breed. Daily play 15-20 min. Long, dense coat — brushing 4-7x/week to prevent mats, especially around the rear and behind the ears. Slow to mature (4-5 years). Typical lifespan 12-15 years.

## Persian

Low-energy brachycephalic. Daily play 10 min. Long coat needs daily brushing without exception, plus weekly face wiping (tear staining). Typical lifespan 12-17 years.

## Siamese

High-energy, vocal, social. Multiple play sessions per day totaling 30+ min. Short coat, weekly brushing. Suffers when left alone — may need a feline companion. Typical lifespan 15-20 years.

## Ragdoll

Medium-energy, docile. Daily play 15 min. Semi-long coat, brushing 3-4x/week. Tend toward obesity — strict portion control. Typical lifespan 12-17 years.

## Bengal

Very high-energy. Daily play 20-30 min, often needs leash walks or large vertical space. Short pelted coat, weekly brushing. Bored Bengals are destructive; food puzzles and climbing structures are essential. Typical lifespan 12-16 years.

## Sphynx

Hairless. No brushing — instead, weekly bath or full-body wipe-down to remove skin oils. Heat-seeking; needs warmth. Typical lifespan 8-14 years.

## Russian Blue

Medium energy, reserved. Daily play 10-15 min. Short dense coat, weekly brushing. Sensitive to routine changes. Typical lifespan 15-20 years.
