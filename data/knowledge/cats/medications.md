## Flea and tick prevention

Topical or oral flea preventives are typically given monthly, year-round, even for indoor-only cats — fleas can hitchhike in on humans and dogs. Many products are not interchangeable between cats and dogs (permethrin in particular is toxic to cats). Recommended frequency=monthly, priority=high, duration=2 min.

## Heartworm prevention

Cats can get heartworm and there is no effective treatment in cats — only prevention. Monthly preventives are recommended in heartworm-endemic areas. Recommended frequency=monthly, priority=high, duration=2 min.

## Daily prescription medications

Cats with chronic conditions (hyperthyroidism, kidney disease, asthma, diabetes) often need medications 1-2 times per day at consistent times. Insulin in particular needs to be given within a 30-minute window of mealtime. Each administration is 2-3 minutes. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=high, duration=3 min.
