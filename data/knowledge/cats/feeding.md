## Adult cat meal frequency

Most adult cats do best on 2-3 small meals per day rather than free-feeding, which often leads to obesity. Each feeding takes 5-10 minutes. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=high, duration=5-10 min.

## Wet food vs dry food

Wet food provides hydration that cats — descended from desert ancestors — often fail to seek out from a water bowl. A diet of at least 50% wet food reduces the risk of urinary issues, especially in male cats. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=high, duration=10 min.

## Kitten feeding

Kittens under 6 months need 4 meals per day; 6-12 months need 3 meals; after 12 months can transition to 2-3 meals. Free access to kitten food is acceptable in this stage because their growth demands are high. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=high.

## Fresh water

Fresh water should be available at all times. Many cats prefer running water (a fountain) to a still bowl, and drink noticeably more from one. Refilling and rinsing the bowl daily takes 2-3 minutes. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=high, duration=2-3 min.
