## Daily play sessions

Indoor cats need 2-3 short play sessions per day (10-15 minutes each) with prey-style toys (wand toys, throw toys). Without daily play, indoor cats commonly develop obesity, anxiety, or aggressive play directed at humans. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=medium-high, duration=15 min.

## Litter box maintenance

Litter boxes should be scooped at least once daily; full litter changes every 1-2 weeks depending on litter type. The general rule is one box per cat plus one extra. Scooping takes 3-5 minutes. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=high, duration=5 min.

## Vertical space and scratching

Cats need vertical territory (cat trees, shelves) and scratching surfaces (vertical and horizontal). This isn't a scheduled task but a setup item; check and rotate toys every few weeks. Recommended frequency=monthly check, priority=low, duration=10 min.

## Solo enrichment

For cats home alone during work hours, food puzzles and window perches reduce boredom-driven destructive behavior. Setting these up takes about 5-10 minutes per day. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=medium, duration=10 min.
