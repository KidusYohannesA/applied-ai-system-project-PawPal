## Brushing — short-haired cats

Short-haired cats (Domestic Shorthair, Russian Blue, Bengal, Siamese, Burmese) benefit from brushing 1-2 times per week to reduce shed and hairballs. Each session takes 5-10 minutes. Recommended frequency=weekly, priority=low-medium, duration=10 min.

## Brushing — long-haired cats

Long-haired cats (Persian, Maine Coon, Ragdoll, Norwegian Forest Cat, Birman) need daily brushing to prevent painful mats. Mats often form behind the ears, in the armpits, and around the rear. Each session takes 10-15 minutes. Recommended frequency=daily, priority=medium, duration=10-15 min.

## Nail trimming

Indoor cats need nail trims every 2-4 weeks because they don't wear down naturally. Each trim is 5-10 minutes when the cat tolerates handling. Recommended frequency=monthly, priority=medium, duration=10 min.

## Teeth brushing

Daily teeth brushing is ideal but rarely tolerated; aim for 2-3 times per week with cat-specific toothpaste. Each session is 2-5 minutes. Recommended frequency=weekly+, priority=medium, duration=5 min.

## Bathing

Most cats do not need routine baths and find them stressful. Exceptions: senior or obese cats that can't groom themselves, and Sphynx (hairless) cats which need a wipe-down or bath every 1-2 weeks. Recommended frequency=monthly (only for needed cats), priority=low.
