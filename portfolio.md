## What this project says about me

This project demonstrates my ability to take an existing application and extend it with new features while maintaining its core functionality. It also shows my understanding of AI concepts such as RAG and LLMs, as well as my ability to work with external APIs and libraries. Crucially, this project is built around the principle that AI agents are tools, not decision makers. Every suggestion requires human approval, outputs are validated and citation-checked, and the app remains fully functional without AI, because responsible engineering means never letting an agent act beyond its role as an assistant.
